## 2. Single reader task and version decision

### Primary search intent

This is a **decision/selection guide**, not an exhaustive spell catalogue: help a player using the **2024 Basic Rules** choose a legal, useful level-5 Warlock spell loadout under two separate constraints—**six base prepared-spell entries on the level-5 Warlock table (spells made always prepared by another feature are separate) and two level-3 Pact Magic slots**—then decide when an upcast changes the result and when concentration or action economy makes a tempting choice a poor fit.

The article must answer one reader task:

> “I am playing a 2024 Warlock. Given my level-5 spell budget and the jobs my party still needs, which small set of spells should I prepare, what should I spend my two Pact Magic slots on, and how can I check that the choices are legal and usable at the table?”

The level-5 case is the worked example—not the keyword’s only valid level—and not a claim that every Warlock is level 5. The method must be reusable at another level by rechecking that level’s table row; the article must not invent a full all-level recommendation table without additional evidence.

### Why this is the one intent

- The supplied English research summary (G01), backed by the recorded US sample, shows four list/database results and one selection discussion. The list pages provide names but do not by themselves solve the limited-choice problem; the discussion asks about upcasting value and scarce spell slots. That supports a selection method rather than another long name list.
- G02 supplies a concrete information gap: 2024 upcasting changes different things—Hex duration, Invisibility/Fly target count, Dispel Magic’s automatic threshold, while Misty Step has no listed upcast improvement in the verified text.
- G03 supplies the second decision gap: two Pact Magic slots are not two simultaneous concentration effects. A player needs both a slot budget and a concentration budget.
- G04 makes the version gate necessary: 2014 uses Spells Known, while 2024 uses the Prepared Spells table and has a different set of text/conditions. The article therefore chooses 2024 and gives only a short version warning, not a parallel 2014 guide.
- G05 confirms that the supplied evidence is sufficient for a level-5 2024 worked example, but not for a complete all-level or all-expansion optimization guide.
