# Scroll paper source records

The fifteen PNG files in this directory are byte-preserved downloads of the paper backgrounds used by the competitor's scroll creator. The competitor's `scrollBgs.css` maps `#scroll1` through `#scroll15` to these source files. They are kept locally so the tool does not hotlink at runtime.
