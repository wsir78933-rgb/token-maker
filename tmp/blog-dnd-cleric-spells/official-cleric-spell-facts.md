# Official Cleric spell facts (2014 vs 2024)

Retrieved: 2026-09-12. Read-only verification. Not a blog draft.

Scope: Cleric **spells and spellcasting**, not the whole class. Claims below are only those a primary page supported. Community rankings are in a separate ADVICE section.

Label note (D&D Beyond editor’s note, 2026-03-02): D&D Beyond now labels 2024 content as “5.5e” and 2014 content as “5e”. This file still uses 2014 / 2024 because that is how the pages themselves are titled.

## How to read a fact

Each fact has:

- `id`
- `claim` — one sentence
- `source` — exact URL opened
- `quote` — exact or close paraphrase of the supporting passage
- `version` — 2014 / 2024 / both / license
- `limitation` — SRD vs full book, free rules vs PHB, 2014 vs 2024

If two sources conflict, both facts are kept. This file does not pick a winner.

## A. Where the official Cleric pages live

### F-SRC-01

- **claim:** On 2026-09-12, `https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/cleric` is the 2014 Legacy Cleric, not the 2024 class.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/cleric
- **quote:** Page title/header: “Cleric Legacy This doesn't reflect the latest rules and lore.” The Spellcasting section uses Wisdom modifier + cleric level to prepare spells and lists Divine Domain at 1st level.
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** This URL is the 2014 class hub. It is not the 2024 Free Rules Cleric.

### F-SRC-02

- **claim:** The 2024 Free Rules (also presented as D&D Beyond Basic Rules) still publish a full Cleric class, including Spellcasting, a Cleric spell list, and the Life Domain subclass.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** Heading “Cleric.” Core traits list Primary Ability Wisdom. Feature “Level 1: Spellcasting” points to the Cleric spell list later in the class description. After the spell list, “This section presents the Life Domain subclass.”
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules
- **limitation:** Free Rules include only one Cleric subclass (Life Domain). Other 2024 PHB domains are not in this free chapter.

### F-SRC-03

- **claim:** The 2014 Basic Rules are still published on D&D Beyond and still include the Cleric class and a Cleric spell list.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes and https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/spells
- **quote:** Classes chapter heading “Cleric” with Spellcasting and Divine Domain. Spells chapter heading “Cleric Spells” with cantrips through 9th-level name lists.
- **version:** 2014 Basic Rules
- **limitation:** 2014 Basic Rules are a free reading product, not SRD 5.1. They coexist with the 2024 Free Rules.

### F-SRC-04

- **claim:** Wizards stated that the 2024 D&D Free Rules will coexist with the 2014 Basic Rules rather than replace them.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1804-start-playing-today-with-the-2024-d-d-free-rules
- **quote:** “The 2024 D&D Free Rules will coexist with the 2014 Basic Rules in the D&D Beyond compendium and will serve as a free entry point to the revised and expanded ruleset.”
- **version:** both
- **limitation:** Official product announcement, not the class rules text.

### F-SRC-05

- **claim:** The 2024 Free Rules are not SRD 5.2; they are a separate free-to-play resource.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1804-start-playing-today-with-the-2024-d-d-free-rules
- **quote:** “The 2024 D&D Free Rules are not SRD 5.2. They are separate materials with different purposes. Where the 2024 D&D Free Rules are a free resource to play fifth edition D&D, the Systems Reference Document serves as a foundation for your fifth edition D&D-compatible creations.”
- **version:** 2024 / license
- **limitation:** Legal/product distinction. Later posts say D&D Beyond replaced the name “Free Rules (2024)” with “D&D Beyond Basic Rules” after SRD 5.2 shipped; the URL path `/sources/dnd/br-2024/` still hosts that 2024 free text.

### F-SRC-06

- **claim:** D&D Beyond’s Creator FAQ says the D&D Beyond Basic Rules cannot be used as a creation license; only the SRD can.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/creator-faq
- **quote:** “Although you will find overlap between the D&D Beyond Basic Rules and SRD 5.2.1, only SRD 5.2.1 is being released under a Creative Commons license. The D&D Beyond Basic Rules cannot be used in content creation.”
- **version:** license
- **limitation:** This is about republication/creation rights, not about whether a player may read the free rules.

## B. How Cleric prepared spells work — 2014

### F-2014-01

- **claim:** In 2014, Wisdom is the Cleric’s spellcasting ability.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your cleric spells. The power of your spells comes from your devotion to your deity.”
- **version:** 2014 Basic Rules (same wording on https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/cleric)
- **limitation:** 2014 only.

### F-2014-02

- **claim:** In 2014, a Cleric prepares a number of cleric spells equal to Wisdom modifier + Cleric level, minimum one, and those spells must be of a level for which the Cleric has spell slots.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “You prepare the list of cleric spells that are available for you to cast, choosing from the cleric spell list. When you do so, choose a number of cleric spells equal to your Wisdom modifier + your cleric level (minimum of one spell). The spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** Formula is in Basic Rules / Legacy class / SRD 5.1. Full 2014 PHB uses the same formula; this file verified it on Basic Rules and the Legacy class page.

### F-2014-03

- **claim:** SRD 5.1 uses the same 2014 prepare-count formula as the Basic Rules Cleric.
- **source:** https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/DND/SRD-OGL_V5.1.pdf
- **quote:** “You prepare the list of cleric spells that are available for you to cast, choosing from the cleric spell list. When you do so, choose a number of cleric spells equal to your Wisdom modifier + your cleric level (minimum of one spell).”
- **version:** 2014 SRD 5.1
- **limitation:** SRD 5.1 is the licensed 2014 rules text. It is not the 2024 class.

### F-2014-04

- **claim:** In 2014, a 3rd-level Cleric with Wisdom 16 prepares six 1st- or 2nd-level spells in any combination.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “For example, if you are a 3rd-level cleric, you have four 1st-level and two 2nd-level spell slots. With a Wisdom of 16, your list of prepared spells can include six spells of 1st or 2nd level, in any combination.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** Example assumes Wisdom 16 (+3) + level 3 = 6.

### F-2014-05

- **claim:** In 2014, casting a prepared spell does not remove it from the prepared list.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “If you prepare the 1st-level spell cure wounds, you can cast it using a 1st-level or 2nd-level slot. Casting the spell doesn’t remove it from your list of prepared spells.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** 2014 only; 2024 example is similar but uses a table count.

### F-2014-06

- **claim:** In 2014, a Cleric can change the entire prepared list after a long rest, and preparing the new list takes at least 1 minute per spell level for each spell on the list.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “You can change your list of prepared spells when you finish a long rest. Preparing a new list of cleric spells requires time spent in prayer and meditation: at least 1 minute per spell level for each spell on your list.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** The 1-minute-per-spell-level prayer time is 2014 class text. 2024 class text on the Free Rules page does not restate this timer.

### F-2014-07

- **claim:** In 2014, Cleric cantrips are **known**, not prepared from the daily list, and the number known is the Cantrips Known column (3 at 1st, 4 at 4th, 5 at 10th).
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “At 1st level, you know three cantrips of your choice from the cleric spell list. You learn additional cleric cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Cleric table.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** 2014 Basic Rules / Legacy class do not grant a cantrip swap when you gain a Cleric level.

### F-2014-08

- **claim:** In 2014, domain spells are always prepared and do not count against the number of spells you can prepare each day.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “Once you gain a domain spell, you always have it prepared, and it doesn’t count against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you have a domain spell that doesn’t appear on the cleric spell list, the spell is nonetheless a cleric spell for you.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** Domain is chosen at 1st level in 2014.

### F-2014-09

- **claim:** In 2014 Basic Rules, the only fully printed Cleric domain is Life Domain, even though the Divine Domain feature names seven PHB domains.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** Divine Domain: “Choose one domain related to your deity: Knowledge, Life, Light, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, or War.” Later: only “Life Domain” is given a full writeup with a Life Domain Spells table.
- **version:** 2014 Basic Rules
- **limitation:** Naming Knowledge/Light/Nature/Tempest/Trickery/War is not the same as printing those subclasses. Full 2014 PHB prints the other domains; Basic Rules print Life only. SRD 5.1 also prints Life Domain only.

### F-2014-10

- **claim:** 2014 Life Domain always-prepared spells are Bless and Cure Wounds (1st); Lesser Restoration and Spiritual Weapon (3rd); Beacon of Hope and Revivify (5th); Death Ward and Guardian of Faith (7th); Mass Cure Wounds and Raise Dead (9th).
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** Life Domain Spells table: 1st bless, cure wounds; 3rd lesser restoration, spiritual weapon; 5th beacon of hope, revivify; 7th death ward, guardian of faith; 9th mass cure wounds, raise dead.
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** Life Domain only. Other 2014 domains have different always-prepared lists in the full PHB, not verified from Basic Rules.

### F-2014-11

- **claim:** In 2014, a Cleric can cast a cleric spell as a ritual if it has the ritual tag and is prepared.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “You can cast a cleric spell as a ritual if that spell has the ritual tag and you have the spell prepared.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** This is a class feature in 2014. 2024 moves ritual casting into the general Spells chapter.

### F-2014-12

- **claim:** In 2014, Clerics use a holy symbol as a spellcasting focus.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** “You can use a holy symbol … as a spellcasting focus for your cleric spells.”
- **version:** 2014
- **limitation:** Same idea exists in 2024 Free Rules.

## C. How Cleric prepared spells work — 2024 Free Rules

### F-2024-01

- **claim:** In 2024 Free Rules, Wisdom is the Cleric’s spellcasting ability.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Cleric spells.” Core Cleric Traits table: “Primary Ability | Wisdom”.
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules
- **limitation:** Free Rules / 2024 PHB family. Not 2014.

### F-2024-02

- **claim:** In 2024 Free Rules, level 1+ prepared Cleric spells are a **table number**, not Wisdom modifier + level.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “To start, choose four level 1 spells from the Cleric spell list. … The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Cleric levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Cleric Features table.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** This is a rules change versus 2014. Wisdom no longer increases how many level 1+ spells you prepare.

### F-2024-03

- **claim:** The 2024 Free Rules Cleric Features table starts with 4 prepared spells at level 1 and ends with 22 at level 20 (Prepared Spells column).
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** Cleric Features table rows include: level 1 Prepared Spells 4; level 2 = 5; level 3 = 6; level 4 = 7; level 5 = 9; level 6 = 10; level 7 = 11; level 8 = 12; level 9 = 14; level 10 = 15; level 11 = 16; level 12 = 16; level 13 = 17; level 14 = 17; level 15 = 18; level 16 = 18; level 17 = 19; level 18 = 20; level 19 = 21; level 20 = 22. Cantrips column: 3 until level 4 (then 4), 5 from level 10.
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules
- **limitation:** HTML table rendering on the page is messy; the Prepared Spells integers above are taken from the visible row values on that page (also matching the same table on `/sources/dnd/free-rules/character-classes`).

### F-2024-04

- **claim:** In 2024, prepared level 1+ spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots; a level 3 Cleric’s six prepared spells can mix levels 1 and 2.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you’re a level 3 Cleric, your list of prepared spells can include six spells of levels 1 and 2 in any combination.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Same mixing rule as 2014; the **count** of six at level 3 is now from the table, not from Wisdom.

### F-2024-05

- **claim:** In 2024 Free Rules, you can replace any of your prepared level 1+ Cleric spells when you finish a Long Rest.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change your list of prepared spells, replacing any of the spells there with other Cleric spells for which you have spell slots.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** The 2024 class feature does not restate the 2014 “1 minute per spell level” prayer timer.

### F-2024-06

- **claim:** The 2024 general Spells chapter agrees that a Cleric may change any prepared spells after a Long Rest.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells/
- **quote:** Spell Preparation by Class table: Cleric — “Finish a Long Rest” — “Any”.
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Table is general; the Cleric class feature is the specific rule for which spells you may choose.

### F-2024-07

- **claim:** In 2024, Cleric cantrips are **known** (not part of the daily prepared level 1+ list), you start with three, and you can replace one cantrip whenever you gain a Cleric level.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “You know three cantrips of your choice from the Cleric spell list. … Whenever you gain a Cleric level, you can replace one of your cantrips with another cantrip of your choice from the Cleric spell list. When you reach Cleric levels 4 and 10, you learn another cantrip of your choice from the Cleric spell list, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Cleric Features table.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Cantrip swap-on-level-up is a 2024 change versus the 2014 Basic Rules text, which only adds cantrips at listed levels.

### F-2024-08

- **claim:** In 2024, always-prepared spells from another Cleric feature do not count against the Prepared Spells number, but they still count as Cleric spells.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “If another Cleric feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don’t count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Cleric spells for you.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Same mechanical idea as 2014 domain spells.

### F-2024-09

- **claim:** The 2024 general rule is that an always-prepared spell does not count against a changeable prepared list.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells/
- **quote:** “Certain features might give you a spell that you always have prepared. If you also have a list of prepared spells that you can change, a spell that you always have prepared doesn’t count against the number of spells on that list.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** General chapter; subclasses and feats supply the actual always-prepared names.

### F-2024-10

- **claim:** 2024 Life Domain (Free Rules) always has listed spells prepared starting at specified Cleric levels, and the subclass itself is gained at Cleric level 3.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “Level 3: Cleric Subclass — You gain a Cleric subclass of your choice.” Life Domain Spells: “When you reach a Cleric level specified in the Life Domain Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Free Rules print only Life Domain. Other 2024 PHB domains are not in this free chapter.

### F-2024-11

- **claim:** 2024 Free Rules Life Domain always-prepared spells are Aid, Bless, Cure Wounds, Lesser Restoration (Cleric 3); Mass Healing Word, Revivify (5); Aura of Life, Death Ward (7); Greater Restoration, Mass Cure Wounds (9).
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** Life Domain Spells table rows as named above.
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules Life Domain
- **limitation:** Compared with 2014 Life Domain, Spiritual Weapon, Beacon of Hope, Guardian of Faith, and Raise Dead are **not** on this 2024 Life always-prepared table. Aid, Mass Healing Word, Aura of Life, and Greater Restoration **are**.

### F-2024-12

- **claim:** In 2024 Free Rules, ritual casting is a general spell rule (prepared + Ritual tag), not a separate Cleric class feature paragraph.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells/
- **quote:** “Certain spells have the Ritual tag in the Casting Time entry. … To cast a spell as a Ritual, a spellcaster must have it prepared.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** 2024 Cleric Spellcasting on the class page does not reprint a “Ritual Casting” heading. Functionally a Cleric still rituals prepared ritual-tagged spells.

### F-2024-13

- **claim:** In 2024 Free Rules, on a turn you can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells/
- **quote:** “On a turn, you can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell. This rule means you can’t, for example, cast a spell with a spell slot using the Magic action and another one using a Bonus Action on the same turn.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** This is the 2024 replacement for the 2014 bonus-action spell restriction. A slotless cantrip can still share a turn with one slotted spell.

### F-2024-14

- **claim:** 2024 recommended starting Cleric cantrips are Guidance, Sacred Flame, and Thaumaturgy; recommended starting 1st-level prepared spells are Bless, Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, and Shield of Faith.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “Guidance, Sacred Flame, and Thaumaturgy are recommended.” “Bless, Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, and Shield of Faith are recommended.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** Recommendations, not required picks. They **are** on the Free Rules Cleric list (see list facts below).

## D. Cleric spell list location and legal citation

### F-LIST-01

- **claim:** The 2024 Free Rules print a complete Cleric spell list (names, school, C/R/M tags) inside the Cleric class, not only in a separate spells chapter.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** “This section presents the Cleric spell list. The spells are organized by spell level and then alphabetized… In the Special column, C means the spell requires Concentration, R means it’s a Ritual, and M means it requires a specific Material component.”
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules
- **limitation:** This is the **free** Cleric list. It is not a license to republish (see F-SRC-06). Full 2024 PHB may add names not on this list.

### F-LIST-02

- **claim:** The 2014 Basic Rules print a Cleric Spells name list in Chapter 11, then full descriptions alphabetically.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/spells
- **quote:** Chapter 11: “The chapter begins with the spell lists of the spellcasting classes.” Heading “Cleric Spells” then cantrip through 9th-level names.
- **version:** 2014 Basic Rules
- **limitation:** Free to read on D&D Beyond. Not the SRD license.

### F-LIST-03

- **claim:** 2024 spell descriptions in Free Rules mark class lists in parentheses after school, e.g. Command is “(Bard, Cleric, Paladin)”.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells/ and https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions
- **quote:** “If a spell is on a class’s spell list, the class’s name appears in parentheses after the spell’s school of magic.” Example from spell descriptions: “Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric, Paladin)” for Command; “Level 6 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)” for Sunbeam.
- **version:** 2024 Free Rules
- **limitation:** Parentheses on a Free Rules spell page only prove Free Rules class membership.

### F-LIST-04

- **claim:** D&D Beyond said the 2024 Free Rules include 333 spells, fewer than the paid 2024 Player’s Handbook.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1804-start-playing-today-with-the-2024-d-d-free-rules
- **quote:** “The 2024 D&D Free Rules also includes 16 feats, 48 monsters, and 333 spells.”
- **version:** 2024
- **limitation:** 333 is the Free Rules spell-description count, not a statement that the Cleric list is complete versus the paid PHB.

### F-LIST-05

- **claim:** SRD 5.2.1 is the Creative Commons document for 2024-rules-compatible publication; SRD 5.1 remains for 2014-rules-compatible publication.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd
- **quote:** Overview table: design/publish using 5e (2014) ruleset → SRD 5.1; design/publish using 5.5e (2024) ruleset → SRD 5.2. “SRD 5.2 is provided to you under Creative Commons” (CC-BY-4.0). English download: SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf (Published May 01, 2025).
- **version:** license
- **limitation:** SRD 5.2 is **not** the entire 2024 PHB. The FAQ says some classes, species, and monsters are excluded.

### F-LIST-06

- **claim:** SRD 5.2’s own FAQ lists 20 spells **added** versus SRD 5.1; that added list includes Aura of Life and Power Word Heal, and does **not** list Toll the Dead, Circle of Power, or Power Word Fortify.
- **source:** https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd
- **quote:** Spells section “Added 20 Spells:” Aura of Life, Charm Monster, Chromatic Orb, Dissonant Whispers, Divine Smite, Dragon’s Breath, Elementalism, Ensnaring Strike, Hex, Ice Knife, Mind Spike, Phantasmal Force, Power Word Heal, Ray of Sickness, Searing Smite, Sorcerous Burst, Starry Wisp, Summon Dragon, Tsunami, Vitriolic Sphere.
- **version:** 2024 SRD 5.2
- **limitation:** “Added versus 5.1” is not the Cleric class list. A spell already in SRD 5.1 (e.g. Sunbeam) can still **move onto** the 2024 Cleric list without appearing in this added-spells bullet.

### F-LIST-07

- **claim:** What is legally citable as **rules text** for a third-party blog/product is SRD 5.1 (2014) and/or SRD 5.2.1 (2024) under their licenses, plus names/facts a writer states without copying non-SRD description text.

## G. 2024 vs 2014 differences that change **which spells you pick** (verified on page)

Only differences verified on an official page are listed. Editorial posts are marked as editorial.

### F-DIFF-PREP-COUNT

- **claim:** 2024 Clerics prepare a fixed table number (4 at level 1); 2014 Clerics prepare Wisdom modifier + level. That changes how many picks you get at a given Wisdom.
- **source:** 2014 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes ; 2024 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** 2014: “Wisdom modifier + your cleric level (minimum of one spell).” 2024: “To start, choose four level 1 spells… number of spells on your list increases… Prepared Spells column.”
- **version:** both
- **limitation:** Counts match only for some Wisdom/level pairs. Using the two formulas on the same pages: Wis 16 (+3) at level 1 is 4 vs 4; at level 5 is 8 vs 9; at level 20 is 23 vs 22. Wis 20 (+5) at level 1 is 6 vs 4. There is no single “2024 always prepares more/less” claim.

### F-DIFF-CANTRIP-SWAP

- **claim:** 2024 Clerics may replace one known cantrip whenever they gain a Cleric level; 2014 Basic Rules only add cantrips at listed levels and do not state a swap.
- **source:** 2024 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes ; 2014 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes
- **quote:** 2024: “Whenever you gain a Cleric level, you can replace one of your cantrips…” 2014: “You learn additional cleric cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column.”
- **version:** both
- **limitation:** 2014 Tasha’s optional cantrip-swap was not opened (marketplace). Do not claim 2014 never allowed a swap under optional rules; claim only that Basic Rules / Legacy class text does not grant it.

### F-DIFF-SUBCLASS-LEVEL

- **claim:** 2014 domain (and its always-prepared spells) starts at 1st level; 2024 subclass (and Life Domain always-prepared spells) starts at 3rd level.
- **source:** 2014 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes ; 2024 https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes
- **quote:** 2014: “Your choice grants you domain spells and other features when you choose it at 1st level.” 2024: “Level 3: Cleric Subclass.” Life Domain Spells begin at Cleric level 3.
- **version:** both
- **limitation:** This changes when always-prepared domain spells free up prepared slots. 2024 levels 1–2 have no domain list.

### F-DIFF-LIFE-DOMAIN-SPELLS

- **claim:** 2024 Life Domain always-prepared list replaces 2014’s Spiritual Weapon / Beacon of Hope / Guardian of Faith / Raise Dead with Aid / Mass Healing Word / Aura of Life / Greater Restoration (Bless, Cure Wounds, Lesser Restoration, Revivify, Death Ward, Mass Cure Wounds remain in some form).
