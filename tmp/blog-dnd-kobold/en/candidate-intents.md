# Candidate intents — US `dnd kobold` cluster

Role: A-EN. Date: 2026-09-16.  
These are **candidates** for role B. No final primary intent is selected here.

Evidence base: ego-browser Google US SERPs (`hl=en&gl=us&pws=0`) plus opened destination pages. Wikidot bodies, BoLS, Dungeon Mister, and D&D Beyond species deep pages were **not** opened successfully; they cannot carry a candidate by themselves.

Do **not** treat “use tokenmaker.one” as a reader job unless the query already is a token/brand query. Head queries `dnd kobold` / `kobold 5e` / `playable kobold 5e` are rules and lore, not a token editor.

---

## Candidate 1 — Explain what a D&D kobold *is* (monster + lore)

**One sentence:** A reader wants a clear picture of kobolds as small draconic/reptilian dungeon dwellers who win with traps, numbers, and Pack Tactics—not a full character sheet.

**Supporting queries and result types**

- `dnd kobold`, `kobold 5e`, `kobold dnd 5e`
- Organic: D&D Beyond 2014 monster, Roll20 Basic Rules (2014) monster, Forgotten Realms Wiki, DDO “Meet the Monsters”, old r/DnDBehindTheScreen ecology
- PAA: “How do kobolds work in D&D?”, “Are kobolds good or evil in D&D?”, “Did D&D invent kobolds?”, “Do kobolds have skin or scales?”
- Related: height, names, age, dragonshield, stat block

**Reader task:** Identify the creature, alignment/type shift, and why a fight against “just CR 1/8” still hurts.

**Looks like:** concept explanation + monster-bestiary, not a tutorial and not token making.

**Version trap this intent must respect:** 2014 `Small Humanoid (Kobold), Lawful Evil` vs 2024 `Kobold Warrior` `Small Dragon, Neutral` (opened D&D Beyond pages). FR wiki already shows both.

## Candidate 2 — Confirm they are a playable race/species and which book to use

**One sentence:** A reader wants to know whether they can play a kobold, and which official PC package their table is actually using (Volo Legacy vs MotM).

**Supporting queries and result types**

- `playable kobold 5e` (this query’s whole SERP)
- Head-query PAA: “Can you play D&D as a kobold?”
- `kobold 5e` / `kobold dnd 5e` PAA: “Are kobolds playable in 5e?”
- Related: `DnD kobold race`, `Kobold 5e species`, `Kobold race 5e Monsters of the Multiverse`, `Kobold dnd 5e 2014`, `DnD 5e Kobold player race`
- Organic: Wikidot *lineage* title (body 未取得), Reddit “Kobold race info”, D&D Beyond forum “Kobold Player Race?”, D&D 5e Wiki race page, RPGBOT version map
- D&D Beyond species search (site, not Google top five): MotM card + Volo Legacy card; no 2024 PHB species card visible

**Reader task:** Pick a legal species source for character creation and know what is Legacy.

**Looks like:** race/species explainer with a version switch, not a monster stat block and not a token tutorial.

**RPGBOT opened claim (third party, not Wizards):** MotM is the last dedicated PC update; 2024 play would keep MotM traits and take ASI from Background. Official 2024 PHB species page: **未核验**.

## Candidate 3 — Look up the 5e stat block to run them

**One sentence:** A DM wants numbers (AC, HP, CR, Pack Tactics, sunlight, weapons) to drop kobolds on a map tonight.

**Supporting queries and result types**

- `kobold 5e`, `kobold dnd 5e`, related `stat block`, `Kobold warrior 5e`, `Winged kobold 5e`, `Dnd kobold dragonshield`
- Organic: D&D Beyond monster, Roll20 Compendium, 5esrd.com creature reprint (opened; third-party SRD site)
- 2024 official page opened: D&D Beyond `Kobold Warrior` (AC 14, HP 7, still Pack Tactics + Sunlight Sensitivity)

**Reader task:** Copy or compare the correct CR 1/8 block for the edition on the table, and know winged/inventor/dragonshield are different monsters.

**Looks like:** monster bestiary / reference, not a PC build guide.

**Do not merge with Candidate 2.** Google currently ranks the *lineage* (playable) Wikidot URL next to the *monster* D&D Beyond URL for the same head queries. That confusion is the gap.

## Candidate 4 — Build a kobold player character (class / optimization)

**One sentence:** A player already knows kobolds can be PCs and wants a class, ASI, and “is this any good?” answer.

**Supporting queries and result types**

- PAA: “What is the best class to build a kobold in D&D 5e?”, “What class is best for kobold?”
- Related: `Kobold dnd 5e build`, `Best playable kobold 5e`, `Playable kobold 5e stats`
- Organic: Norse Foundry “Let’s Build A Kobold!”, RPGBOT class tables (MMoM / custom origin / classic), D&D Beyond forum builds, GiantITP stats thread (SERP only)

**Reader task:** Choose a class that survives Small size + the version’s traits (Volo Pack Tactics vs MotM Draconic Cry).

**Looks like:** race-build guide / optimization, not a lore essay.

**Caveat:** Norse Foundry’s Pack Tactics wording did not match the opened 2014 monster text. Any article in this intent must not copy that page’s numbers blindly.

## Candidate 5 — Get a kobold token for VTT or the table

**One sentence:** A user who typed `token` wants art or a circular PNG, not Kurtulmak lore.

**Supporting queries and result types**

- `kobold token`: **MTG-first** (Time Spiral Remastered token, Kobolds of Kher Keep, Scryfall, Gatherer) with a minority of VTT packs
- `dnd kobold tokenmaker.one`: tokenmaker.one homepage **and** blog index rank; remaining hits are still token art/packs
- Related on bare `kobold token`: `Token maker`, Kher Keep Commander terms

**Reader task:** Find or make a small-reptile token that reads at map scale.

**Looks like:** token making / asset shopping. **Not** the intent of `dnd kobold` or `kobold 5e` as captured.

**Brand note:** tokenmaker.one appears because the query contains the domain. It is a SERP fact for the brand query only. Do not retrofit it onto Candidates 1–4.
