## H2: Pack Tactics is why a CR 1/8 pack still hurts

**Job:** “How do kobolds work?” as a table action, not lore.

**Figure FIG-02** here (countable 5-ft grid). See `figure-plan.md`.

### H3: Advantage from an ally within 5 feet

Worked example, labeled example not a session log: two kobolds adjacent to one fighter → both attacks at advantage if the ally is not incapacitated. One kobold alone → no Pack Tactics. Do not write Norse Foundry’s once-per-rest / 10-ft version. Do not write MotM Draconic Cry here.

### H3: Sunlight is not the same sentence in both books

2014: attacks and sight-based Wisdom (Perception) at disadvantage in sunlight.  
2024 Warrior: **ability checks and attack rolls** at disadvantage in sunlight.  
Example: a 2014 lookout still makes a non-Perception check without that trait’s sunlight penalty; a 2024 Warrior does not get that gap. Do not average.

### H3: Small still takes one square

SRD both years: Small = 5×5 ft = 1 square, same as Medium. Space is the control zone, not a 5-foot-wide body. A corridor holds a pack the same way it holds Medium creatures. Tiny (4 per square) is not this creature.

Foundry/Roll20, one sentence max if VTT is already in the reader’s head: occupancy is 1 grid unit; Foundry `dynamicTokenScale` 0.8 resizes **art**, not the square. Do not turn this H3 into software setup.

Traps: one sentence — they compensate with traps and numbers. Not a trap workshop. Not 2024 MM unique ecology.
