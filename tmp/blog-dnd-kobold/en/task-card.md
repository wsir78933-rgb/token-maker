4. Ability scores Str 7 / Dex 15 / Con 9 / Int 8 / Wis 7 / Cha 8 match across both monster entries. Shared CR, speed 30, Darkvision 60, Common + Draconic are the mix-trap, not proof the blocks are the same.

6. SRD 5.1 p.92 and SRD 5.2.1 size table: Small = 5×5 ft = 1 square.
