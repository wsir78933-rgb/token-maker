## Two hard vetoes

1. **Family Crest / icons / Generator / AI / 家族纹章 / 家徽.** Live group 9 already fails this: title leads with Family Crest, description says icons, and the rest of the body says charges/symbols/family banners. Those words are uncaught claims. Generator/AI is also a lie for a manual editor.

2. **Always-restore drafts, cloud sync, official/authentic heraldry, surname lookup, “in minutes”, user counts, or unmeasured catalog/template counts.** Draft restore is only “if the same browser still has a recent draft.” Export is on-device, not a synced account. Do not sharpen past those facts.
