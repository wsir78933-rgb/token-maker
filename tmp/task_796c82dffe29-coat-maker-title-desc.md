# Coat of Arms Maker — 3 title/description pairs

Read-only proposal. No product files changed.
Counts: EN = `len()`. ZH weighted = Han ideograph 2, all other characters 1.
Keyphrases: EN `Coat of Arms Maker` / `coat of arms maker`. ZH `纹章制作器`.

## 1) Three groups

### Group 1 (recommended)

- **EN title (H1):** Coat of Arms Maker: Free in the Browser
  - chars: 39 (band 15–60)
- **EN desc (intro):** Edit shields, field patterns, charges, text, and layers in this coat of arms maker. Free in the browser, no account; export PNG, JPEG, or PDF on your device.
  - chars: 157 (band 70–160); exact keyphrase once
- **ZH title (H1):** 纹章制作器：免费在浏览器中编辑
  - weighted: 29 (band 15–60)
- **ZH desc (intro):** 用这个纹章制作器编辑盾牌、底纹、图形、文字和图层。浏览器里免费使用，无需账号，可在设备上导出 PNG、JPEG 或 PDF。
  - weighted: 103 (band 70–160); exact keyphrase once
- **Page facts:** browser editor (shields, field patterns, charges, text, layers); free / no paid plan; no account; project in browser; on-device PNG, JPEG, PDF export
- **Why SERP + on-page:** Keyword is first (fixes live group 9), Free matches the SERP winners we can honestly claim, and the same string works as an H1 plus a first paragraph that tells a lander what they edit and how they leave with a file — without Family Crest, icons, Generator, or AI.

  - chars: 157 (band 70–160); exact keyphrase once

  - chars: 40 (band 15–60)

## 2) Recommended group

**Group 1.** It is the only pair that leads with the primary keyphrase, keeps the winning Free + in-browser offer, and still reads as a tool-page H1/intro. Group 2 is a valid audience variant but narrower. Group 3 is a valid CTA variant but weaker on the Free SERP cue in the title.

## 3) Two hard vetoes

1. **Family Crest / icons / 家徽** in title or description. Live group 9 already fails this: the rest of the page says charges, symbols, and family banners. Title=H1 would put a label the editor never uses.
2. **Generator, AI, “in minutes”, official heraldry, surname lookup, always-restore drafts, user counts, or unmeasured catalog counts.** Those are either explicit do-not-promise items or an H1 that describes a product we do not ship (a generator, not this editor).
